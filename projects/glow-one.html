Glow

A private-label beauty line for noon — and the naming strategy that gave it a name of its own. Instead of a generic "Beauty" label, the work carves out a distinct, contemporary identity that can stand beside dedicated players in the category.

noon
Role

The brand

noon is one of the region's largest e-commerce platforms, and "beauty" is already a major category on the app. So a private-label beauty range couldn't simply be called Beauty — it needed its own name, its own presence, and a reason to be chosen on a shelf full of trusted brands.

Private label is usually read as the low-cost compromise next to the main draw. In beauty especially, value lives in the brand behind the bottle. The brief, then, was to build a label that feels intentional and current — a glow-led identity that earns its place rather than undercutting it.

Thought process

We started from a simple tension: private-label beauty is only worth as much as the brand you build around it. Rather than lean on the parent category, we gave the line a distinct name so it could be positioned with its own character.

Names were explored in two buckets. Direct names cue the category and benefit instantly — quick to read, but harder to own. Thematic names carry more playfulness and edge, and let the line sit alongside dedicated players; the individual product names (Sebum Remover, Face Serum, and so on) do the work of establishing category context, so the master brand is free to have personality. That thinking pointed toward a glow-forward route — warm, confident, and unmistakably beauty.
